FAQ

Quick answers

How do I pay?

You pay after the cleaning is finished. We'll confirm payment options with you on WhatsApp when we confirm your booking.

How many hours do I need?

As a guide, a regular clean of a 1–2 bedroom apartment takes about 3–4 hours with one cleaner. Deep cleans and larger homes take longer, or go faster with two cleaners. Not sure? Book your best guess and add a note, and we'll advise when we confirm.

What if I need to change or cancel?

Just message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we'll move or cancel your slot.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. Leave access instructions in the notes and we'll arrange it with you when we confirm.

What's included in "with materials"?

Our cleaners bring the cleaning products and tools needed for the job, so you don't have to buy anything.
